# Lab 04: Alert Triage Tabletop

**Status:** Practice exercise — scenario is fictional.

## Objective
Practise prioritizing alerts based on evidence, privilege, scope, potential impact, and confidence.

## Scenario
A fictional monitoring queue contains:
- Repeated failed logins against a privileged account.
- An unexpected PowerShell process alert on a workstation.
- A large outbound transfer from a finance workstation.
- Several user-reported phishing emails.

## Triage approach
For each alert, record:
1. **Severity and rationale:** What is the possible impact?
2. **Evidence:** What logs, process details, destination information, or user reports support the alert?
3. **Scope:** Which user, host, account, or systems may be affected?
4. **False-positive checks:** What benign explanations should be tested?
5. **Next action:** What additional evidence should be collected?
6. **Escalation:** Who should be informed under the incident-response playbook?
7. **Containment:** Recommend only authorized actions under the organization's process.
8. **Documentation:** Record timestamps, evidence, decisions, and uncertainty.
