## Why document a lab?

A useful lab write-up should show not only what you did, but why you did it and what the evidence means. It should help your future self repeat the work and help another reader understand the result.

## A repeatable structure

1. **Objective:** State the question or skill being practised.
2. **Environment:** Record the tools and setup.
3. **Procedure:** List the steps clearly enough to repeat.
4. **Evidence:** Include relevant output, screenshots, logs, or packet details.
5. **Analysis:** Explain what the evidence indicates and what it does not prove.
6. **Conclusion:** Summarise findings and remaining questions.

## Keep claims evidence-based

Separate observed facts from interpretation. If an event looks suspicious, record the indicators and the checks you performed instead of declaring it malicious without enough evidence.

## Next steps

Use this structure for your next lab and adapt it when a topic needs a different format.
